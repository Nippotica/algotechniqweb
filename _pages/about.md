Algotechniq delivers production-ready computer vision systems for 
industrial and infrastructure applications. From traffic safety 
monitoring to manufacturing quality control, our solutions transform 
visual data into actionable intelligence on Nvidia platforms.

> Algotechniq = Physical AI for Industrial Deployment

We specialize in harnessing deep learning and computer vision to enhance 
defect detection and quality control in manufacturing, while automating 
the real-time monitoring of critical infrastructure. Our solutions range 
from standalone software to comprehensive enterprise systems, tailored to 
diverse deployment needs.

From initial design through post-delivery support, our end-to-end services 
ensure we address the unique demands of every project. We regularly 
collaborate with leading scientists at UC Berkeley, ETH Zurich, UTokyo, 
and Keio University to ensure our clients benefit from the latest AI 
innovations.
